Research

Three threads, one question: which genes let wheat hold its ground when the environment turns against it, and how can we prove what they do.

Heat stress · protein function

TaPUB16, a heat-responsive U-box E3 ligase

I am characterising TaPUB16 from bread wheat on two fronts. In vitro, I express and purify the protein using MBP (pMAL-c5x), His (pET32a) and GST (pGSTag) fusion systems to set up biochemical assays. In planta, I test TaPUB16-overexpressing Arabidopsis lines (OE8, OE10) against wild type under 42 °C heat stress, refining the design to one plant per pot for cleaner statistics.

  • Protein expression
  • Transgenic Arabidopsis
  • Thermotolerance assays
Plant pathology · RDA project

Wheat response to Fusarium head blight

Since April 2025 I have worked on a Rural Development Administration project on Fusarium head blight. I inoculate wheat spikes with Fusarium asiaticum spores and follow disease-related gene expression by qRT-PCR, linking host molecular responses to infection progress.

  • Spore inoculation
  • qRT-PCR
  • Host–pathogen interaction
Transcriptomics · in progress

Heat time-course in wheat seedlings

I am designing a time-course experiment on two-week-old wheat seedlings: RNA sampled at up to seven time points after heat treatment, with four biological replicates, to map when stress-responsive genes such as the PUB family switch on and off.

  • Experimental design
  • RNA extraction
  • Gene expression dynamics

Looking ahead
